Two Font Awesome Free icons are used, converted from their SVG path data into
Android vector drawables. The shapes are unchanged; they are only scaled and
positioned for each use:

  Krate brand mark (app icon, in-app logo)
    app/src/main/res/drawable/ic_krate.xml
    app/src/main/res/drawable/ic_launcher_foreground.xml
    app/src/main/res/drawable/ic_launcher_monochrome.xml

  Notification icon
    app/src/main/res/drawable/ic_stat_krate.xml

The app credits Font Awesome on its About page.
